# Logo decision — sushi-finder

Status: **OPEN** — no winner selected. Owner picks.

All five marks are real Grok Imagine brand art, keyed to true transparent PNGs
(RGBA, corner alpha 0 except where subject intentionally reaches the canvas
edge on mark-04). Not emoji, not letter monograms, not SVG scribbles.

## Candidates

| # | File | Concept | Notes from generation review |
|---|------|---------|------------------------------|
| 1 | `mark-01.png` | **Nigiri** — single tuna nigiri with wasabi | Instant category read. Soft dimensional shading, glossy coral fish on rice. Holds on light and dark; ivory rice is slightly low-contrast on pure white at 24px, strong at 32–200px. |
| 2 | `mark-02.png` | **Maki cross-section** — nori ring, rice, salmon/avocado center | Best geometric small-size silhouette of the set. Reads as an app icon at 24–32px without mush. Strong concentric form. |
| 3 | `mark-03.png` | **Fish map pin** — teal location pin with coral fish | Product-metaphor mark (finder + place). Bold solid forms; favicon-friendly. Teal body holds on light and dark. |
| 4 | `mark-04.png` | **Chopsticks lift** — wood chopsticks holding a nori-banded salmon nigiri | Most narrative / premium of the five. Diagonal composition has more empty space; at 24px the chopsticks thin out more than marks 2–3. Strong at 48–200px and marketing sizes. |
| 5 | `mark-05.png` | **Mon crest** — indigo double ring, coral koi, wave | Japanese mon / badge direction. Dual-tone indigo + coral. Transparent exterior (keyed). Holds at 24px as a circle-with-fish; detailed scales reward 200px. |

## Explicit naming

**mark-05** is the mon crest (indigo circular ring, coral koi over waves). It is
listed as a candidate only — not chosen, not shortlisted by default.

- Transparent background on every file (verified: PNG RGBA; corners alpha 0 on marks 1–3 and 5; mark-04 chopsticks reach the canvas edge by design).
- Sized and inspected at 24 / 32 / 48 / 240px in `gallery.html`.
- Proof composites also under `proof/` (light and dark at 24 / 32 / 240).
